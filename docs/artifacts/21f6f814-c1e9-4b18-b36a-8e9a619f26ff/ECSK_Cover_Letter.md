We submit our manuscript entitled **"Observational Constraints on Spacetime Torsion: CMB, Compact Objects, and Gravitational Wave Signatures in Einstein-Cartan-Sciama-Kibble Theory"** for consideration as an Original Research Article in *Physical Review D*.

Einstein-Cartan-Sciama-Kibble (ECSK) theory provides a natural, geometric extension of General Relativity by relaxing the symmetry of the connection, allowing spacetime torsion to couple algebraically to the intrinsic spin density of matter fields. At extreme densities, this coupling generates a repulsive gravitational effect that replaces cosmological and black hole singularities with nonsingular bounces. While the theoretical foundations of ECSK theory are well-studied, placing robust, quantitative constraints on the torsion coupling parameters using real astronomical datasets remains a major gap in the literature.

In this work, we present a unified observational test suite for ECSK theory across three distinct physical regimes:

1.  **Cosmological CMB Acoustics:** We evaluate the acoustic peak shifts of the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) using a modified bounce cosmology. Crucially, we identify a **non-analyticity cusp** at the General Relativity baseline ($\eta_{\text{tor}} = 0$) caused by the comoving sound horizon integration terminating at the bounce redshift ($z_{\text{bounce}} \propto \eta_{\text{tor}}^{-1/2}$). We show that this square-root scaling ($\Delta \ell \propto \sqrt{\eta_{\text{tor}}}$) renders standard Fisher matrix forecasting invalid at the GR boundary. To resolve this, we perform a direct $\chi^2$ likelihood profiling against the **Planck 2018** observed peak locations, yielding a $1\sigma$ ($2\sigma$) upper limit on the cosmological torsion parameter:
    $$\eta_{\text{tor}} < 3.30 \times 10^{-16} \quad \left(\eta_{\text{tor}} < 7.21 \times 10^{-16}\right)$$
2.  **Astrophysical Hydrostatic Equilibrium:** We solve the modified Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff (TOV) equations under unpolarized spin-torsion coupling. Using X-ray mass-radius bounds from the **NICER** mission for the massive pulsar **PSR J0740+6620** ($M \ge 2.01 M_{\odot}$), and calibrating a stiffened baseline nuclear equation of state ($K=180.0$), we establish a strict upper bound on the core-softening spin-torsion coupling:
    $$\eta < 29.25$$
    We further discuss the degeneracies between gravitational torsion softening and standard nuclear physics EoS uncertainties.
3.  **Dynamic Core Collapse:** We simulate the 1D spherically symmetric collapse and bounce of a stellar core using Lagrangian General Relativistic hydrodynamics under the Misner-Sharp metric. Applying the quadrupole formula, we extract the gravitational wave strain $h(t)$ of the core bounce, demonstrating a unique, asymmetric **double-peaked strain signature** that provides a clear template for next-generation detectors (Einstein Telescope and Cosmic Explorer) to distinguish torsion from standard singular collapse.

Given the relevance of these results to the gravitational physics and astrophysics communities, we believe this manuscript is a strong fit for *Physical Review D*.
